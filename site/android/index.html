Install ATA on your Android phone

Free, and it takes about two minutes. You download the ATA install file from this page and open it. The first time, your phone asks you to allow it. That's expected, and the steps below show what to tap.

Download ATA for your phone

For Android 9 or later. Not sure? Settings → About phone.

Reading this on your Mac computer? Open the camera on your Android phone and point it at this code. This page opens on your phone, with the download button.

Or type this address in your phone's browser: magiclin99.github.io/ata/android

Step by step

The words on your phone may differ slightly, depending on its brand and Android version.

  1. Download. On your phone, tap Download ATA for your phone at the top of this page. If your browser asks whether to download or keep the file, choose to download it.
  2. Open the file. When the download finishes, tap Open in the message at the bottom of the screen. Missed it? Open your phone's Downloads or My Files app and tap the ATA file (ATA.apk).
  3. Allow your browser to install apps (first time only). Your phone says something like "For your security, your phone currently isn't allowed to install unknown apps from this source." Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back. This only lets the app you downloaded with (for example Chrome) install apps; you can turn it off again afterwards.
  4. Install. Your phone asks "Do you want to install this app?" Tap Install.
  5. If Google Play Protect offers to scan ATA, let it. Your phone hasn't seen ATA before, so it may suggest scanning it first. Choose to scan. After a short wait, it shows the result and you can finish installing. If it says the app is harmful, stop and email us.
  6. Open ATA. Tap Open. Then, with ATA open on your Mac, tap Pair with a Mac and scan the QR code on the Mac's screen. No ATA on your Mac yet? Install it on your Mac.
Samsung phones: if your phone says the install was blocked by Auto Blocker, open Settings → Security and privacy → Auto Blocker and turn it off. Install ATA, then turn Auto Blocker back on. Samsung turns this on by default on newer phones, and blocks every app that isn't from an app store, not just ATA.

Updating ATA later

ATA on your phone doesn't update by itself. To get the newest version, download it again from this page and open the file, as in steps 1, 2 and 4. Your phone asks "Do you want to update this app?" Tap Update (or Install). It installs over the old one: your paired Macs and your list of sent files are kept.

If you turned "Allow from this source" off, your phone asks again. On Samsung phones with Auto Blocker on, turn it off for a moment, as above.
