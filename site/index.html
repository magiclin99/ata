How it works

  1. STEP 1

    Install on both

    ATA on your Mac, and ATA on your Android phone.

  2. STEP 2

    Pair once

    Open ATA on your Mac and it shows a QR code. Scan it with ATA on your phone. You only do this once.

  3. STEP 3

    Share

    In any app, tap Share → Send to Mac. The file lands in your Mac's Downloads folder.

Good to know

Free, for real

No purchases, no ads, no limits, no account.

Private

No cloud: files go straight from your phone to your Mac, encrypted the whole way. ATA collects nothing about you.

Keep them close

Your phone needs to be near your Mac, in the same room, with Bluetooth on. It can't send to a Mac somewhere else.

Same Wi-Fi is fastest

On the same Wi-Fi, a 60 MB video takes about 6 seconds. If your phone is on mobile data, it still works, but slower, and it uses your data.

Works with an Android phone (Android 9 or later) and a Mac with macOS 14 or later. Not with iPhone, and only from phone to Mac. More questions

Support ATA (optional)
