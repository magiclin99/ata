Help and feedback

Something not working, or an idea for ATA? Here's how to get an answer quickly.

1. Check the common fixes

  • The phone can't find the Mac: make sure ATA is running on the Mac, Bluetooth is on for both devices, and the phone is close to the Mac (about the same room).
  • "No direct path": put the phone on the same Wi-Fi as the Mac and try again. Some office, school, and public networks block direct connections.
  • Pairing failed or expired: the QR code is valid for 5 minutes and works once. Choose Pair New Device… on the Mac again for a fresh code.
  • The Mac app won't open: follow the Open Anyway steps, or install with the one-line command.

More answers are in the FAQ.

2. Report a problem or suggest something

3. What to include

These details usually make the difference between a quick fix and a long back-and-forth:

  • Phone: model and Android version (Settings → About phone).
  • Mac: macOS version (Apple menu → About This Mac) and the ATA version on both devices.
  • Which step failed: pairing, finding the Mac, connecting, or during the transfer. Include the exact message the app showed, or a screenshot.
  • Network: were both devices on the same Wi-Fi? If not, what was the phone on (mobile data, another Wi-Fi)? Was it a home, office, school, or public network?
  • Does it happen every time? And roughly how large were the files?

Please don't send the pairing QR code, the pairing link, or private files. They aren't needed.

No logs are sent automatically

ATA has no crash reporting or analytics, so nothing about a failure reaches the developer unless you write in. That also means your report is the only way a problem gets noticed. Only what you choose to include in an issue or email is shared.
