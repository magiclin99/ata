Help

Something not working, or have an idea? Try these first, then write to us.

1. Quick fixes

  • Your phone can't find your Mac: make sure ATA is open on the Mac, Bluetooth is on on both, and your phone is close to the Mac (same room).
  • It says it couldn't reach your Mac: connect your phone to the same Wi-Fi as your Mac and try again.
  • Sending is very slow: see the firewall fix.
  • The QR code doesn't work: each one works once, for 5 minutes. On the Mac, click Show a New QR Code, or Pair a Phone… in the ATA window, for a new one.
  • ATA won't open on your Mac: follow step 3 of the install guide.
  • ATA says "Move ATA to Applications first": drag ATA onto the Applications folder in the download window, then open it from Applications.

More answers: Questions.

2. Write to us

Email magiclin99@gmail.com

Have a GitHub account? You can also report problems or ideas on GitHub, where others can see and add to them.

3. What to include

If you can, tell us:

  • Phone: model and Android version (Settings → About phone).
  • Mac: macOS version (Apple menu → About This Mac) and the ATA version on both devices.
  • What happened: while pairing, finding the Mac, or sending? Include the exact message the app showed, or a screenshot.
  • Wi-Fi: were both on the same Wi-Fi? If not, was the phone on mobile data? At home, at work, or on public Wi-Fi?
  • Does it happen every time? And roughly how large were the files?

Please don't send a picture of the pairing QR code, or any private files. We don't need them.

We only know what you tell us

ATA doesn't send us anything automatically, not even error reports. So if something goes wrong, your message is the only way we find out. Thank you for writing.
