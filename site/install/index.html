Install ATA on your Mac

It takes about two minutes. The first time you open ATA, your Mac shows a security warning. That's expected, and this page shows exactly what to click.

You need macOS 14 or later. Not sure? Click the Apple menu in the top-left corner → About This Mac.

1. Download

Download ATA for Mac

Free. Works on any Mac with macOS 14 or later.

2. Move ATA into Applications

  1. Open the file you just downloaded (ATA-1.0.2.dmg, in your Downloads folder).
  2. A small window opens with the ATA icon and an Applications folder. Drag ATA onto Applications.

3. Open ATA (and allow it once)

Open the Applications folder and double-click ATA. Your Mac will say it can't check ATA. That's because ATA isn't from the App Store and wasn't submitted to Apple's paid check. You only need to allow it once. Follow the steps for your macOS version:

macOS 14Sonoma

  1. You'll see this message. Click OK.
  2. In the Applications folder, hold Control and click ATA (or right-click it), then choose Open.
  3. A similar message appears, this time with an Open button. Click Open.

macOS 15 or later

  1. When the message appears, click Done. Don't click "Move to Trash".
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, and scroll down. Next to the message that ATA was blocked, click Open Anyway.
  3. Click Open Anyway again, and enter your Mac password (or use Touch ID).

We checked the macOS 14 screens on a real Mac. The macOS 15 button names come from Apple's own instructions. If the Open Anyway button is missing, double-click ATA once more and look again (it only shows for about an hour).

4. Allow, then pair your phone

  1. When your Mac asks if ATA may use Bluetooth, click Allow. ATA uses it so your phone can find this Mac.
  2. If your Mac asks whether ATA may accept incoming network connections, or find devices on your network, click Allow. That's what makes sending fast.
  3. In the ATA window, click Pair New Device… and a QR code appears.
  4. On your phone, open ATA, tap Pair with a Mac, allow what it asks, and point the camera at the QR code.
Done! Now try it: on your phone, open a photo, tap Share → Send to Mac. The photo appears in your Mac's Downloads folder. (The first time, ATA may be hidden under "More" in the share menu.)

Keep ATA ready

  • ATA has to be open on the Mac to receive files. Closing its window is fine: it keeps running quietly.
  • In the ATA window, turn on Launch at Login so it's ready after you restart your Mac.
  • To bring the window back, open ATA again from Applications.

Another way: install with Terminal (no security warning)

If you're comfortable typing a command, this installs ATA without the warning in step 3.

  1. Open Terminal: press Command + Space, type Terminal, and press Return.
  2. Copy this line, paste it into Terminal, and press Return: 
    curl -fsSL https://github.com/magiclin99/ata/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sh
  3. If it asks for a password, type the password you use to log in to your Mac (nothing appears while you type), then press Return.
  4. ATA opens by itself. Continue with step 4 above.

The installer checks that the download is the genuine, unmodified ATA before installing. Details are on the Technical details page.

Updating and removing

  • Update: in ATA, click Check for Updates…, download the new version, and drag it into Applications again (choose Replace). ATA doesn't update by itself.
  • Remove: in the ATA window, click Quit ATA, then drag ATA from Applications to the Trash.
