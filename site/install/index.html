Install ATA on your Mac

It takes about two minutes. The first time you open ATA, your Mac shows a security warning. That's expected, and step 3 shows exactly what to click.

You need macOS 14 or later. Not sure? Click the Apple menu in the top-left corner → About This Mac.

1. Download

Download ATA for Mac

Free. Works on any Mac with macOS 14 or later.

2. Drag ATA into Applications

Open the ATA file you just downloaded (in your Downloads folder). In the window that opens, drag ATA onto the Applications folder, following the arrow.

If you open ATA straight from this window, it asks you to move it to Applications first.

3. Open ATA (and allow it once)

Open the Applications folder and double-click ATA. Your Mac will say it can't check ATA. That's because ATA isn't from the App Store and wasn't submitted to Apple's paid check. You only need to allow it once. Follow the steps for your macOS version:

macOS 14Sonoma

  1. You'll see this message. Click OK.
  2. In the Applications folder, hold Control and click ATA (or right-click it), then choose Open.
  3. A similar message appears, this time with an Open button. Click Open.

macOS 15 or later

  1. When the message appears, click Done. Don't click "Move to Trash".
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, and scroll down. Next to the message that ATA was blocked, click Open Anyway.
  3. Click Open Anyway again, and enter your Mac password (or use Touch ID).

We checked the macOS 14 screens on a real Mac. The macOS 15 steps follow Apple's own instructions and haven't been checked on a real Mac yet; if you see something different, please tell us. If the Open Anyway button is missing, double-click ATA once more and look again (it only shows for about an hour).

4. Pair your phone

ATA opens with a QR code already showing.

  1. When your Mac asks if ATA may use Bluetooth, click Allow.
  2. On your phone, open ATA, tap Pair with a Mac, allow what it asks, and scan the big QR code. No ATA on your phone yet? Scan the small QR code with your phone's camera to get it.
  3. If your Mac then asks whether ATA may accept incoming network connections, click Allow. It only asks if your Mac's firewall is on. On macOS 15 or later it may also ask whether ATA may find devices on your local network. Click Allow there too. (We haven't checked this on a real Mac yet.)
Done! Now try it: on your phone, open a photo, tap Share → Send to Mac. The photo appears in your Mac's Downloads folder. (The first time, Send to Mac may be under "More" in the share menu.)

Keep ATA ready

  • When you pair your first phone, ATA turns on Open at Login, so it's ready after you restart your Mac. You can switch it off in the ATA window.
  • Closing the ATA window is fine: ATA keeps running. To see the window again, click ATA in the Dock.

Another way: install with Terminal (no security warning)

If you're comfortable typing a command, this installs ATA without the warning in step 3.

  1. Open Terminal: press Command + Space, type Terminal, and press Return.
  2. Copy this line, paste it into Terminal, and press Return: 
    curl -fsSL https://github.com/magiclin99/ata/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sh
  3. If it asks for a password, type the password you use to log in to your Mac (nothing appears while you type), then press Return.
  4. ATA opens by itself. Continue with step 4 above.

The installer checks that the download is the genuine, unmodified ATA before installing. Details are on the Technical details page.

Updating and removing

  • Update: ATA doesn't update by itself. Download it again with the button in step 1, drag it into Applications, and choose Replace. Then allow it once more, as in step 3. Your paired phones and settings are kept. (Or run the Terminal command above again.)
  • Remove: in the ATA window, click Quit ATA, then drag ATA from Applications to the Trash.
