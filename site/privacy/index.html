Privacy Policy

Short version: ATA collects no data about you and sends nothing to us or anyone else. Your files go directly from your phone to your own Mac. The only outside contact: when your phone and Mac aren't on the same Wi-Fi, a connection helper run by Google or Cloudflare sees your network address (section 3).

Applies to: the ATA Android app (io.github.magiclin99.ata) and the ATA Mac app. Effective date: November 15, 2026. Developer: magiclin99, contact magiclin99@gmail.com. 中文版在下方.

1. What the developer collects

Nothing. ATA has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no crash-reporting service. Neither app sends any personal data, usage data, file data, or device identifiers to the developer or to third parties. The only exception is in section 3: when the devices aren't on the same Wi-Fi, a connection helper run by Google or Cloudflare sees your devices' network addresses. Both apps are open source (MIT) at github.com/magiclin99/ata, so you can check these statements against the code.

2. Data that stays on your devices

  • Pairing information. When you pair, each device stores a random device ID, the other device's name, and a shared 256-bit pairing key. On Android, the key is encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore, and app backups are turned off. On the Mac, it's stored in a file in ATA's Application Support folder that only your macOS user account can read, and that folder is excluded from Time Machine backups. This information never leaves your two devices.
  • Device names. During pairing and each transfer, your phone sends its device name to your Mac, and the Mac sends its computer name to your phone, so each side can show who it's talking to.
  • Recent transfers. The Android app keeps a local list of recent transfers (file names, sizes, times, and results) so you can see what you sent. The Mac app remembers where its last 8 received files were saved, for its Recent Files menu, and you can clear that list. Uninstalling the Android app deletes its list.
  • Your files. Files you choose to share are read on the phone and written to the folder you chose on your Mac (Downloads by default). ATA keeps no other copy.

3. How a transfer travels

Bluetooth

The Mac app uses Bluetooth (Bluetooth Low Energy) so your paired phone can find it nearby. While it does, it broadcasts a short name, "ATA-" followed by 8 characters of a random ID, which any nearby Bluetooth device can see. It contains nothing personal. After connecting, the two devices prove they were paired with each other, and everything after that is encrypted. On Android 12 and later, ATA declares that it doesn't use Bluetooth to determine location. On Android 9 to 11, Android requires the Location permission to look for Bluetooth devices; ATA doesn't read or use your location.

Same Wi-Fi

When your phone and Mac are on the same Wi-Fi, the file goes directly over that Wi-Fi, encrypted (AES-256-GCM) with a key that only your two devices have. Nothing goes to the internet.

Different networks

When they aren't on the same Wi-Fi, the two devices connect to each other directly over the internet (WebRTC). To find each other, each device first asks a public connection helper run by Google or Cloudflare (technically a STUN server: stun.l.google.com, stun.cloudflare.com) for its own network address. Those helpers see your devices' network addresses (IP addresses and ports), as any internet service you contact would. They never receive your files, file names, device names, or keys, and they handle that request under Google's and Cloudflare's own privacy policies. Your phone and Mac also learn each other's network addresses, which they need in order to connect. The connection is encrypted (DTLS). ATA never uses a relay server (TURN), so your files never pass through any server.

4. Camera and Google Play on Android

  • Camera (QR code scanning). ATA asks for the Camera permission only when you tap scan to pair with a Mac. The QR code is decoded on the phone by an open-source decoder built into the app. Camera frames aren't saved, aren't sent anywhere, and are discarded once the code is read. ATA keeps only the text of the pairing link. If you deny it, ATA can't pair by QR code; it tells you so and offers a button to open settings.
  • Google Play statistics. Google Play may give developers aggregate install statistics, and crash and performance reports from users who opted in to sharing diagnostics with Google. That's controlled by your Google and Android settings, not by ATA.

5. Mac app network use

Apart from receiving transfers as described above, the Mac app makes no network requests. Check for Updates… opens the GitHub Releases page in your web browser. The one-line installer and release downloads come from GitHub, and this website is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub may log your IP address under GitHub's Privacy Statement. This site uses no cookies, analytics, or third-party scripts.

7. Your choices and deleting data

Because the developer holds no data about you, there's nothing to request, export, or delete from the developer. To remove everything ATA stores, use Remove pairing in either app, then uninstall the app. On the Mac, you can also delete ATA's folder in ~/Library/Application Support/. Received files are ordinary files in your chosen folder.

8. Children

ATA isn't directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

隱私權政策

簡單說：ATA 不收集任何關於你的資料，也不會傳任何東西給我們或其他人。你的檔案直接從手機傳到你自己的 Mac。唯一的對外接觸：手機和 Mac 不在同一個 Wi-Fi 時，Google 或 Cloudflare 提供的連線輔助服務會看到你的網路位址（見第 3 節）。

適用範圍：ATA Android app（io.github.magiclin99.ata）與 ATA Mac app。生效日期：2026 年 11 月 15 日。開發者：magiclin99，聯絡信箱 magiclin99@gmail.com。English version above.

1. 開發者收集的資料

沒有。ATA 沒有伺服器、沒有帳號、沒有分析追蹤、沒有廣告、沒有內購，也沒有當機回報服務。兩個 app 都不會把任何個人資料、使用資料、檔案資料或裝置識別碼傳給開發者或第三方。唯一的例外見第 3 節：兩台裝置不在同一個 Wi-Fi 時，Google 或 Cloudflare 提供的連線輔助服務會看到你裝置的網路位址。兩個 app 都以 MIT 授權開放原始碼（github.com/magiclin99/ata），你可以對照程式碼檢查這些說法。

2. 只存在你裝置上的資料

  • 配對資訊。配對時，每台裝置會儲存一個隨機的裝置 ID、對方的裝置名稱，以及一把共用的 256 位元配對金鑰。Android 上這把金鑰以 Android Keystore 中的金鑰加密後儲存，並關閉 app 備份；Mac 上則存成 ATA「Application Support」資料夾裡的一個檔案，以檔案權限保護，只有你的 macOS 使用者帳號能讀取，而且這個資料夾不會被 Time Machine 備份。這些資訊不會離開你的兩台裝置。
  • 裝置名稱。配對與每次傳輸時，手機會把手機名稱傳給你的 Mac，Mac 也會把電腦名稱傳給手機，讓雙方知道在跟誰連線。
  • 最近的傳輸。Android app 會在本機保留最近的傳輸紀錄（檔名、大小、時間、結果），方便你查看；移除 app 時會一併刪除。Mac app 會記住最近收到的 8 個檔案存放位置，顯示在「最近的檔案」選單中，可以隨時清除。
  • 你的檔案。你選擇分享的檔案在手機上讀取，寫入你在 Mac 上指定的資料夾（預設「下載項目」）。ATA 不會另外留存副本。

3. 一次傳輸經過哪些地方

藍牙

Mac app 用藍牙（藍牙低功耗）讓已配對的手機在附近找到它。這段期間它會廣播一個短名稱：「ATA-」加上隨機 ID 的 8 個字元，附近的藍牙裝置都看得到，但其中不含任何個人資料。連上之後，兩台裝置會先確認彼此是配對過的，之後的所有內容都經過加密。Android 12 以上，ATA 已宣告不會用藍牙判斷位置；Android 9 到 11 的系統規定找藍牙裝置需要位置權限，ATA 不會讀取或使用你的位置。

同一個 Wi-Fi

手機和 Mac 連同一個 Wi-Fi 時，檔案會直接透過這個 Wi-Fi 傳送，並用只有你兩台裝置才有的金鑰加密（AES-256-GCM），不會連到網際網路。

不同的網路

不在同一個 Wi-Fi 時，兩台裝置會透過網際網路直接互相連線（WebRTC）。為了找到對方，每台裝置會先向 Google 或 Cloudflare 提供的公開連線輔助服務（技術名稱是 STUN 伺服器：stun.l.google.com、stun.cloudflare.com）查詢自己的網路位址。這些服務看得到你裝置的網路位址（IP 位址與連接埠），就像你連到任何網路服務一樣；它們不會收到你的檔案、檔名、裝置名稱或金鑰，並依 Google 與 Cloudflare 各自的隱私權政策處理這個請求。你的手機和 Mac 也會得知彼此的網路位址，這是互相連線所必需的。連線經過加密（DTLS）。ATA 從不使用轉送伺服器（TURN），所以你的檔案不會經過任何伺服器。

4. Android 上的相機與 Google Play

  • 相機（QR code 掃描）。只有在你點選掃描來配對 Mac 時，ATA 才會要求相機權限。QR code 由 app 內建的開源解碼器在手機上解讀；相機畫面不會儲存、不會傳到任何地方，讀到 QR code 後即丟棄，ATA 只保留配對連結的文字。如果拒絕，ATA 就無法用 QR code 配對，會告訴你並提供「開啟設定」按鈕。
  • Google Play 統計。Google Play 可能會提供開發者彙總的安裝統計，以及選擇與 Google 分享診斷資料的使用者的當機與效能報告。這由你的 Google 與 Android 設定控制，與 ATA 無關。

5. Mac app 的網路使用

除了上述的接收傳輸之外，Mac app 不會發出任何網路請求。「檢查更新…」會在你的瀏覽器開啟 GitHub Releases 頁面。一行安裝指令與版本下載來自 GitHub，本網站也架設在 GitHub Pages 上，因此 GitHub 可能依 GitHub 隱私權聲明記錄你的 IP 位址。本網站不使用 cookie、分析工具或第三方指令碼。
