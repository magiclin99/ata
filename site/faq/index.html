Questions

Before you start

Is it really free?

Yes. No purchases, no ads, no limits, and no account. Nothing is locked.

Does it work with iPhone, iPad, or a Windows PC?

No. ATA sends from an Android phone (Android 9 or later) to a Mac (macOS 14 or later). It also only goes one way: phone to Mac, not Mac to phone.

Do my phone and Mac need to be close?

Yes, in the same room. ATA uses Bluetooth to find your Mac, so it can't send to a Mac somewhere else.

Do they need to be on the same Wi-Fi?

No, but it's much faster. On the same Wi-Fi, a 60 MB video takes about 6 seconds. If your phone is on mobile data, sending still works, but it's slower and uses your data.

What can I send, and how big?

Anything you can share from an app: photos, videos, PDFs, documents, several at once. There's no size limit, as long as your Mac has enough free space.

Where do the files go on my Mac?

Into your Downloads folder. You can pick another folder in the ATA window on the Mac. If a file with the same name is already there, ATA keeps both.

Safety and privacy

Is it safe? Can anyone else see my files?

Your files go straight from your phone to your Mac, encrypted the whole way. No cloud and no company server is involved. Only the phone and Mac you paired can connect to each other.

Does ATA collect my data?

No. No account, no tracking, no ads, and nothing is sent to us. The full details are in the privacy policy.

Why does my Mac say it can't check ATA?

ATA isn't from the App Store and wasn't submitted to Apple's paid check, so the first time, your Mac asks you to allow it yourself. The install guide shows what to click. You only do it once.

Why does my phone ask for "Nearby devices", location, or the camera?

Nearby devices lets ATA find your Mac over Bluetooth. ATA doesn't use it to locate you. Some older Android versions call this location instead; ATA still doesn't use your location. The camera is only for scanning the QR code when you pair, and nothing is saved or sent. If you'd rather not, choose "Paste pairing link" instead.

Can I see how it works?

Yes. The code is public, so anyone can check it. See Technical details.

When something doesn't work

My phone can't find my Mac
  • Make sure ATA is open on the Mac. If you're not sure, open ATA from the Applications folder.
  • Turn on Bluetooth on both the phone and the Mac.
  • Bring the phone closer to the Mac.
It says it couldn't reach my Mac

Connect your phone to the same Wi-Fi as your Mac and try again. Some office, school, hotel, and public Wi-Fi networks block this kind of connection when the two devices are on different networks.

Sending is slow, or ATA says the firewall is blocking it

Your Mac's firewall may be stopping your phone from connecting directly. Then sending still works, but slowly. To fix it:

  1. Open System Settings → Network → Firewall → Options…
  2. Find ATA and set it to Allow incoming connections. If ATA appears more than once, select the extra ones and click − so only one is left.
  3. Quit ATA (in the ATA window, click Quit ATA) and open it again from Applications.

If your Mac asks whether ATA may "accept incoming network connections", click Allow. You don't need to turn the firewall off.

It worked yesterday, but not after I restarted my Mac

ATA has to be open on the Mac. Open it from Applications, and turn on Launch at Login in the ATA window so it starts by itself next time.

I can't find ATA in my phone's share menu

Look for Send to Mac. The first time, it may be under More at the end of the list. After you use it, Android usually shows it nearer the front.

The QR code doesn't work

Each QR code works once and only for 5 minutes. On the Mac, click Pair New Device… again for a fresh one.

A transfer stopped halfway

Files that finished are already on your Mac. Just share the rest again.

Can I pair more than one Mac or phone?

Yes. Your phone lists every Mac you've paired and shows which ones are nearby. A Mac can be paired with several phones, but receives from one at a time.
